# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors, whether contributing directly or with AI-assisted tools.

## Safety-First Collaboration
In AIriProject, "safety" extends to both technical integrity and community interaction. 
- **Respectful Interaction:** Treat all community members with respect.
- **Safety Over Speed:** Prioritize safety protocols and governance over rapid feature expansion.
- **Privacy:** Do not share or post sensitive data, secrets, or personal information of others.

## Moderation
Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct. Unsafe or malicious contributions will be moderated immediately.
